# Aviora: business plan (free for travellers)

## Why it can win
- **Visas are the hidden first step for African travellers.** In 2025 about 48% of Nigerian, 47% of Ghanaian and 52% of Senegalese Schengen applications were refused, and Africans paid 42% of the fees lost to refusals (LAGO Collective, European Commission data).
- **No app combines it all:** visa rules for *your* passport, real approval stories, places matched to your vibe, hidden gems you can "feel" first, and a map of your travels.
- **Closest players:** BRDRS/Detty (diaspora travel and fintech), Visadoc (Nigerian visa agency app), Atlys (visa processing, India-first), Wanderlog and Mindtrip (planning, passport-blind), and the "Been" style travel-map apps.

## How it makes money without charging travellers for everything

| Who pays | What they get | Month-12 target |
|---|---|---|
| Partner commissions (stays, flights, rides, eSIM, insurance, tours) | Bookings from travellers who are ready to go | ~$1,500 |
| A bank or fintech sponsor | "Money abroad" placement on every country guide | ~$1,000 |
| Tourism boards (Rwanda, Kenya, Ghana, Mauritius, Cape Verde…) | Featured to travellers whose passport and vibe fit | ~$800 |
| Vetted consultants and agencies, paying per referral | Warm leads from people who just checked a route | ~$800 |
| Optional application review, refunded if it doesn't help | Expert eyes before paying a visa fee | ~$900 |
| **Total** | | **≈ $5,000 / month** |

Plus equity-free grants in year one (for example the Tony Elumelu Foundation, Google for Startups Accelerator Africa, Accelerate Africa).

**One rule:** partners never change visa information, verdicts or rankings, and sponsored content is always labeled.

## Growth
1. **Search:** 139 visa route pages, 36 country guides and 55 gem pages are built in. Add more passports, countries and gems every month.
2. **Community data is the moat:** collect the first 50 visa stories and 200 gem verdicts by hand from friends and travel groups.
3. **Short video:** "Feel it" clips (the scene plus the sound), "visa myth vs fact", and "is it worth it?" verdicts. Every video points to the app.
4. **Partnerships:** one bank and one tourism board in the first six months.

## Costs
Hosting about $0–20, Supabase about $0–25, the Claude API (capped by the daily free-question limit), and a domain.
